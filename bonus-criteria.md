# Bonus Criteria - What to Include/Exclude in Reports

## ✅ INCLUDE - Actual Bonuses Only

### Reload Bonuses
- Deposit match bonuses for existing customers
- Free bets given to existing customers
- Free spins for reload deposits

### Cashback & Rakeback
- Weekly/monthly cashback on losses
- Poker rakeback
- VIP/loyalty cashback

### Enhanced Odds & Boosts
- Daily/weekly odds boosts
- Accumulator boosts (e.g., +10% on winning accas)
- Enhanced odds tokens

### Promotional Offers
- Time-limited reload campaigns
- Event-specific bonus offers (e.g., Derby bonus bets)
- Weekend reload specials

---

## ❌ EXCLUDE - Not Bonuses

### Site Features (NOT bonuses)
- Edit bet function
- Cash out features
- Early payout options
- Bet builder tools
- Streaming services

### Risk Management Tools (NOT bonuses)
- 0-0 refund insurance
- Acca insurance
- Money-back specials on specific outcomes
- "Safe bets" that refund on certain results

### Competitions & Sweepstakes (NOT bonuses)
- Free prize draws
- Prediction competitions
- Trivia contests
- Leaderboard prizes

### Welcome Bonuses (NEVER include)
- First deposit bonuses
- New customer offers
- Sign-up bonuses
- Registration freebets

---

## 🎯 Quick Test: Is it a Bonus?

Ask these questions:
1. **Does it give you free money/value?** → Yes = Potential bonus
2. **Is it for existing customers only?** → Yes = Continue
3. **Is it a standard site feature?** → Yes = EXCLUDE
4. **Does it require you to win a specific outcome?** → Yes = EXCLUDE (insurance/refund)
5. **Is it an automatic percentage boost/cashback?** → Yes = INCLUDE

---

## Examples

### ✅ INCLUDE These:
- "100 kr freebet for depositing 100 kr" → Reload bonus
- "10% rakeback on poker" → Cashback bonus
- "Daily odds boost token" → Enhanced odds
- "+10% on all winning accas" → Accumulator boost
- "Black Week: Bet 100 kr, get 100 kr freebet" → Promotional offer

### ❌ EXCLUDE These:
- "0-0 refund on selected matches" → Insurance/risk management
- "Early payout if team leads by 2 goals" → Site feature
- "Edit your bet after placing" → Site feature
- "Free competition: Win 500,000 kr" → Competition/sweepstakes
- "Acca insurance: Get refund if 1 leg fails" → Insurance

---

## Summary Rule

**Only include offers that give you FREE MONEY or VALUE BOOSTS**
**Exclude insurance, refunds, competitions, and site features**
